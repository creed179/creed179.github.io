The Brief

Why this name.

17 September 2026 · The name

Hampstead Hill, 1814

In the War of 1812, in September of 1814, Baltimore was under siege from the water and from the land. Hampstead Hill sat at the center of the land line. Commodore John Rodgers’s sailors and Marines, the city militia, and thousands of Baltimoreans—including free Black laborers—dug the earthworks and held the ridge. That defense is the name of this firm, and it is how we treat investor capital: protect the capital first, stay on mission, and do not give up the line.

Thesis

Survive the drawdown, compound through the cycle, and keep principal protection ahead of cleverness.
